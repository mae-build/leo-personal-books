Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

Who uses this app. Exactly one person: its owner and operator, Mae Deluca. There are no other users, customers, or accounts.

What data it accesses. Accounting data from the owner’s own QuickBooks Online company through the QuickBooks Online Accounting API: chart of accounts, transactions, customers, vendors, items, and reports.

Where data goes. Data is processed on a single computer owned by the operator, located in the United States. Nothing is sold, shared, or transferred to third parties. Data is not used for advertising or analytics. Short-lived copies may be sent to an AI model provider (Anthropic) under the operator’s account to answer the operator’s own questions; that provider does not train on the data under the account terms in effect.

Credentials. OAuth tokens are stored encrypted at rest in a secrets manager and in files readable only by the operator’s account on that computer. They are never committed to source control.

Retention and deletion. Cached data lives in files the operator controls and can be deleted at any time. Disconnecting the app in QuickBooks Online revokes its access immediately; stored tokens are then deleted.
