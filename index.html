Leo Personal Books

A private integration between one QuickBooks Online company and a locally hosted assistant used only by its owner, Mae Deluca. It is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and is not listed on the Intuit App Marketplace.

The software runs on a computer in the owner’s home in Massachusetts. It reads and writes accounting data in the owner’s own QuickBooks Online company, only when the owner asks it to.
